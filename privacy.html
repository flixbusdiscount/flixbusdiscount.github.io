Privacy policy

What this store collects.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RideCode Deals collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the site, start checkout, buy a digital voucher code, reveal a code after payment, or contact support.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email flixbusdiscount@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Checkout information handled by Stripe, such as payment status, checkout session ID, transaction amount, currency, and receipt details.
  • Support information you send by email, such as your receipt, session ID, route details, screenshots, and issue description.
  • Technical request information handled by hosting and security providers, such as device, browser, network, and request metadata.
  • Coarse location metadata provided with requests, such as country and region, used for currency display and aggregate visit counts.
  • Page path and date for aggregate analytics, such as total visits by country, region, and page.
  • Code delivery information needed to verify paid checkout sessions and reveal the purchased code.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask buyers to create an account.
  • We do not store full payment card numbers or card authentication details.
  • Our custom visit tracker does not store raw IP addresses.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How we use information

  • Process checkout and confirm payment status.
  • Reveal the purchased voucher code after payment verification.
  • Provide support, refunds, replacements, and fraud prevention.
  • Show the appropriate checkout currency and maintain aggregate site analytics.
  • Protect the site, debug technical issues, enforce Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

RideCode Deals uses service providers to operate the store. These may include Stripe for payment processing, Cloudflare for the Worker API, security, and coarse request metadata, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Google/Gmail for support email. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices when you use their services.

Legal bases where required

  • Contract: to process payment, deliver the code, and provide order support.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the site, prevent fraud, maintain analytics, and improve reliability.
  • Legal obligations: to keep records, respond to lawful requests, and handle disputes or chargebacks.
  • Consent: where your location requires consent for a specific optional use.

Retention

  • Stripe transaction records are retained by Stripe according to Stripe policies and legal requirements.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve the request, prevent repeated disputes, and keep reasonable business records.
  • Aggregate analytics counts may be kept for site reporting because they do not identify individual visitors.
  • Code delivery records, if stored, are kept as long as needed to verify delivery, prevent duplicate reveals, and handle refunds or disputes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or information about how your data is used. California residents may also have rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain sensitive information uses, and avoid discrimination for exercising privacy rights. To make a request, email flixbusdiscount@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify the request.

International processing

The site and its providers may process information in countries other than where you live. When required, RideCode Deals relies on available legal mechanisms and provider safeguards for cross-border processing.

Security

RideCode Deals uses Stripe-hosted checkout and serverless hosting to reduce direct handling of payment data. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please contact support if you believe your order information has been misused.

Children

This store is not directed to children. Do not use the checkout if you are not old enough to enter into an online purchase contract in your location.

Changes

RideCode Deals may update this Privacy Policy for future visits or orders. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
